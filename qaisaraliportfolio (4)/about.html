My journey into web development started over three and a half years ago. It didn't take long to find a specialty — the Shopify ecosystem, and the specific challenge of solving complex e-commerce problems inside it.

"I don't rely on bloated third-party apps to solve problems. I engineer custom, app-free native solutions using advanced Liquid and vanilla JavaScript — for elite performance and zero storefront lag."

That early curiosity turned into a dedicated full-time career: architecting native OS 2.0 themes from scratch, building custom backend logic, and falling further in love with the craft of e-commerce engineering along the way.

My mission is simple — help enterprise and luxury D2C brands translate complex business requirements into scalable, lightning-fast storefronts that actively convert.
